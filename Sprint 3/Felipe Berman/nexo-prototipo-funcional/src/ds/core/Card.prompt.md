Standard content surface: `--radius-lg`, hairline border, `--shadow-xs` at rest.

```jsx
<Card interactive>
  <h3 style={{ fontSize: "var(--fs-h3)" }}>Estágio em Design</h3>
  <p style={{ marginTop: 8, fontSize: "var(--fs-body-sm)" }}>São Paulo · Híbrido</p>
</Card>
```

Tones: `light` (default, on white), `tint` (green-50, on white sections), `dark` (green-800, inside green-900 bands), `accent` (green-500 — one per group, for the highlighted item).
